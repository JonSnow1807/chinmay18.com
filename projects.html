Student Scheduler

systems · one-day build

A Flask API that builds a conflict-free timetable for 500 synthetic students with Google OR-Tools CP-SAT: sections, capacity, time conflicts, a 3–5 course load and prerequisites as hard constraints, with a preference-weighted objective. The solver runs under a configured 60-second cap, so the schedule is feasible rather than proven optimal.

500 synthetic students · 1,880 assignments · 0 conflicts by constructionsingle committed run, full_metrics.json; not reproducible from the committed seed (capacities 30–60 in seed_data.py vs 150–250 in the results) · zero conflicts is a hard constraint, not a result
